Grant program help

Money tight? A safer walk-in shower may still be within reach.

If your finances have changed, or if your household income is below the median household income in your state, you may qualify for grant programs or special pricing that can help with a walk-in shower installation.

Contact us today so we can set an appointment, learn what you need, and work with you to see what options may be available. When paperwork is needed, we help guide the process so you are not trying to figure it out alone.

  • Income-based options
  • Grant paperwork guidance
  • Flexible appointment times

Grant programs, special pricing, and eligibility vary by state, income level, documentation, and project scope.
