Disabled veteran assistance

Walk-in shower help may be available for disabled veterans.

If you have a qualifying disability level, there may be government grant programs that can cover part of a walk-in shower project, and in some cases may cover the full shower cost.

We handle the paperwork with you. We just need to set up an appointment, review your situation, and see what programs or special pricing you may qualify for. If a grant is not available, disabled veterans may still qualify for Blue Rhino Bath discounts.

  • Grant paperwork support
  • Special veteran pricing
  • Low-threshold shower options

Program eligibility and award amounts vary by location, agency, documentation, and project scope.
